# Product context

Home Assistant camera controls for older family members. The product is used
on phones and a desktop browser. Its core tasks are choosing a room, viewing
upper/lower pictures, and safely moving supported lower lenses.

Preserve the two-choice Home page, view-only All Cameras page, working continuous
live-video fallback, and honest unsupported-feature labels. Never confuse
display cropping with camera movement or snapshots with continuous video.

No credentials, household addresses, captured footage or app binaries belong in
the public repository. Configuration must remain portable with HA full backups.
